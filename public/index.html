The House

Built for a family that arrived together

Seri Putra is a full terrace house on a quiet stretch of Jalan Megah 10, in an established Taman Megah neighbourhood. You book the whole place — there are no other guests, no shared front door, and no one else's shoes at the entrance.

The downstairs opens into a long living area that runs through to the dining table, so a group of ten can sit together without anyone perching on a chair in the corridor. Upstairs, four bedrooms sleep up to twelve.

  • 3 queen bedrooms + 1 single bedroom
  • Air-conditioning in every room and every shared space
  • Shower room with instant water heater
  • Separate second toilet with hand bidet
  • Dedicated gaming room
  • Smart TV in the main lounge
  • Dining table seating six, fridge, kitchen access
  • Ceiling fans throughout
  • Free parking on the property
  • Towels and bedding provided
